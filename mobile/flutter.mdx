## Stage 1: Foundations (Beginner)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Learn Dart basics">
    Types, null safety, classes, async/await, collections.

    - [Dart language tour](https://dart.dev/language)
    - [DartPad](https://dartpad.dev)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Flutter and build your first app">
    - [Flutter: Get started](https://docs.flutter.dev/get-started)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Widgets and layouts">
    Stateless vs stateful, Row, Column, Stack, ListView, theming.

    - [Widget catalog](https://docs.flutter.dev/ui/widgets)
    - [Flutter codelabs](https://docs.flutter.dev/codelabs)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>**Project:** Counter app, then a to-do app.</Note>

## Stage 2: Real apps (Intermediate)

<Steps>
  <Step title="State management">
    Learn the basics, then pick one solution and stick with it.

    - [State management options](https://docs.flutter.dev/data-and-backend/state-mgmt/options)
    - [Riverpod](https://riverpod.dev)
    - [Bloc](https://bloclibrary.dev)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigation and local storage">
    Routing, passing data between screens, saving data on the device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Networking and REST APIs">
    - [Flutter networking cookbook](https://docs.flutter.dev/cookbook/networking)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find packages">
    - [pub.dev](https://pub.dev)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>**Project:** Notes app with local storage, then an API-driven app.</Note>

## Stage 3: Production-ready (Advanced)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Firebase with FlutterFire">
    Auth, Firestore, push notifications.

    - [FlutterFire](https://firebase.flutter.dev)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Architecture and testing">
    Clean architecture, unit, widget, and integration tests.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Release your app">
    - [Android release guide](https://docs.flutter.dev/deployment/android)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>**Project:** Firebase-backed app with authentication and real-time data.</Note>
