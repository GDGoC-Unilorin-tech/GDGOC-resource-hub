## Stage 1: Foundations (Beginner)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Learn Kotlin basics">
    Variables, functions, null safety, classes, collections, lambdas.

    - [Kotlin docs: Getting started](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/getting-started.html)
    - [Kotlin Playground](https://play.kotlinlang.org)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install Android Studio and build your first app">
    - [Android Basics with Compose](https://developer.android.com/courses/android-basics-compose/course)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn Jetpack Compose">
    Composables, layouts, state, lists, navigation, theming.

    - [Jetpack Compose pathway](https://developer.android.com/courses/jetpack-compose/course)
    - [Compose docs](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>**Project:** Tip calculator, then a to-do list app.</Note>

## Stage 2: Real apps (Intermediate)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Architecture and MVVM">
    ViewModel, UI state, repository pattern.

    - [Guide to app architecture](https://developer.android.com/topic/architecture)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Local storage with Room">
    - [Room documentation](https://developer.android.com/training/data-storage/room)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Networking with Retrofit and Coroutines">
    - [Retrofit](https://square.github.io/retrofit/)
    - [Kotlin Coroutines overview](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/coroutines-overview.html)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Practice with codelabs">
    - [Android codelabs](https://developer.android.com/codelabs)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>**Project:** Notes app (Room), then a news or weather app (Retrofit + MVVM).</Note>

## Stage 3: Production-ready (Advanced)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Dependency injection with Hilt">
    - [Hilt for Android](https://developer.android.com/training/dependency-injection/hilt-android)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Firebase: Auth, Firestore, Storage">
    - [Firebase for Android setup](https://firebase.google.com/docs/android/setup)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Testing, performance, publishing">
    Unit tests, UI tests, app signing, Play Console.

    - [Publish your app](https://developer.android.com/studio/publish)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Next: Kotlin Multiplatform">
    Share logic between Android and iOS.

    - [Kotlin Multiplatform docs](https://kotlinlang.org/docs/multiplatform.html)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>**Project:** Chat or e-commerce app with Firebase auth, Hilt, and clean architecture.</Note>
