## What you will learn

Use browser evidence and manual checks to prioritise improvements.


## Before you begin

Use your repaired practice site, then apply the same checks to your portfolio. Keep the browser version, viewport, and test conditions in your record.

## Understand the idea

Automated audits surface useful signals, but a score does not prove that someone can use your site. Begin with an actual visitor task. A performance result is also tied to the device, network, and cache conditions under which it was measured.

## Worked example

Use only the keyboard to reach each input, submit a calculation, reload the list, and follow a link. Check that labels identify fields and focus remains visible. Use the browser's accessibility information to inspect names and roles. Test at 320 CSS pixels, 200% zoom, and 400% zoom, recording overlap or loss of content.

Run a Lighthouse audit on your local page. Choose one relevant finding, locate the responsible source, make a focused improvement, and rerun under the same settings. For an image issue in your portfolio, inspect its transfer size in Network and its displayed dimensions before choosing a smaller asset. Avoid treating a changed score alone as proof of a meaningful improvement.

Use this record format:

```text
Task: Enter two numbers and read the result using the keyboard.
Environment: browser/version, viewport, zoom, network conditions.
Observed problem: ...
Source cause and repair: ...
Retest result: ...
Not tested: screen-reader interaction unless actually checked.
```

<Note>Device emulation is a useful layout check; it does not reproduce every behaviour of a physical phone or assistive technology.</Note>

## Practice challenge

Perform a keyboard journey and one measured improvement on your portfolio. Keep the original and revised evidence; record any audit items you did not address.

<Accordion title="Need a hint?">

Prioritise a defect that blocks a real task, such as missing focus or an oversized image, instead of chasing every score point.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="View a suggested solution">

A useful report names the element, source rule or asset, and retest. For example: a focus outline was removed; restoring a contrasting focus-visible outline allowed the keyboard user to locate the Add button. Do not claim a screen-reader pass from this observation alone.

</Accordion>

## Completion checklist

- [ ] I recorded a complete keyboard journey.
- [ ] I compared measurements under matching conditions.
- [ ] I stated which checks remain untested.
