**Learn → Practise → Build → Demonstrate → Contribute → Ship**

<Info>The Technical Innovation & Development team must confirm readiness criteria, available tasks, and review responsibilities. Completing a course does not automatically grant production access.</Info>

## Proposed pathway

1. Complete practical checkpoints and a capstone.
2. Demonstrate your work and respond to feedback.
3. Choose an available starter task with the technical team.
4. Submit the change through the project's contribution workflow.
5. Address review feedback and let maintainers approve integration and release.

Possible Resource Hub starter tasks include correcting an explanation, improving an example, checking accessibility, or fixing a broken link. Actual assignments depend on maintainer approval.
