## Task

Audit your explorer with pure-function checks, DOM checks, keyboard testing, and one measured performance trace. Introduce one deliberate defect in a practice copy, show a failing check, then repair it.

Attempt the task without copying a full implementation. Refer back to individual lesson concepts when necessary. Keep this work in a separate practice folder.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] Tests include empty, exact-boundary, and no-match inputs.
- [ ] A deliberate defect is caught before repair.
- [ ] Keyboard and accessibility-state checks are recorded.
- [ ] The trace names the action, input size, and environment.
- [ ] Known limitations are documented without claiming universal accessibility or performance.

## Evidence to keep

Keep your source files and a short record of each input/action, expected result, actual result, and repair. Explain one design choice and one error you investigated. A screenshot supports the record but does not replace runnable code.

<Accordion title="Hint">

Choose a defect such as mutating source order or forgetting the empty state. Record enough detail for another learner to reproduce it.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Review your attempt">

Demonstrate every criterion in your browser and source. Re-run the same checks after each fix. Topic lessons supply worked examples; this checkpoint intentionally asks you to combine them independently rather than copy a complete answer. If a criterion fails, revisit the related lesson before moving on.

</Accordion>

<Note>This checkpoint is self-assessed unless a reviewer examines the evidence. It does not automatically grade or unlock the next page.</Note>
