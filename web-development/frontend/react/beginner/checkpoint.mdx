## Demonstrate your skills

Render at least three unique cards, let visitors save/unsave them, show the saved count, and support saved-only filtering with a useful empty message.

Use your own content and demonstrate one normal journey plus an empty or failure state. Explain which component owns each piece of state. Record the action, expected result, observed result, and repair.

## Completion checklist

- [ ] My example meets each stated requirement.
- [ ] I can explain why state belongs in its chosen owner.
- [ ] I have actual test evidence and a stated limitation.

<Accordion title="Hint">
Reduce the example to three known projects so you can predict counts and order. Change one input at a time when diagnosing a mismatch.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Suggested assessment">
Compare the matching practice-app stage with your version. The observable behaviour should match the brief even if your component names differ. A screenshot alone does not establish that interaction, cleanup, or keyboard behaviour works. Revisit the relevant lesson for any requirement you cannot demonstrate.
</Accordion>
