## Your challenge

Build a portfolio for fellow learners and a technical reviewer. A visitor must be able to understand who you are, explore your work, and see evidence of your learning. Extend your own HTML, CSS, and JavaScript projects. Use fictional details until you choose what to publish.

## Before you begin

Complete the final challenges in [HTML](/web-development/frontend/html/final-project), [CSS](/web-development/frontend/css/final-project), and [JavaScript](/web-development/frontend/javascript/final-project). You should be able to explain semantic structure, responsive layouts, DOM events, modules, fetch, and error handling. Revisit those lessons when a checkpoint exposes a gap.

## Required outcome

- Five connected pages: Home, About, Projects, Learning log, and Practice contact.
- Original content describing at least three projects and your contribution to them.
- Responsive layouts, visible keyboard focus, labelled controls, and usable light and dark themes.
- A project explorer with search, category filters, sorting, reset, and reload.
- Loading, empty, failure, and blocked-storage states; essential content available without JavaScript.
- A local test record, a reviewed preview, and a deployed website with repeatable setup instructions.

<Warning>The practice contact form must clearly say that it does not send messages. Do not present a demonstration as a working backend.</Warning>

## Work through the capstone

| Page | Evidence you will produce |
| --- | --- |
| Integrate your portfolio | A connected HTML/CSS/JavaScript implementation |
| Build milestones | A plan, original content, and completed feature checklist |
| Test and repair | Reproducible checks, fixes, and retests |
| Deploy with Firebase | A tested preview and live URL |
| Demonstrate and review | A submission package and rubric review |

<Note>These are self-assessment and peer-review criteria. The hub does not record completion or automatically unlock courses.</Note>
