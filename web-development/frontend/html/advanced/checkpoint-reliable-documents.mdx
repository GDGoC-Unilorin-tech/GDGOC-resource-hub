## Skill to demonstrate

Apply valid content relationships and appropriate image selection and loading.

Complete this module's lessons before attempting the task. Work from your own understanding first; you may revisit explanations when needed. Keep your checkpoint work alongside your portfolio files.

## Practice materials

Use your existing portfolio files and, when needed, the [supplied local assets](/assets/html/html-practice-assets.zip). Complete the related topic exercises before this checkpoint.

## Your task

Audit your portfolio for invalid nesting and duplicate IDs. Use the supplied 480- and 960-pixel image variants, record their dimensions, and add one responsive image example with a fallback. Explain resolution selection versus art direction and justify loading choices.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] No duplicate IDs or known invalid interactive nesting remain.
- [ ] Image candidate descriptors match actual files.
- [ ] Fallback image and alternative text are present.
- [ ] Dimensions are accurate and loading choices are justified.

## Evidence to keep

- Your HTML files and required local assets.
- A short explanation of your decisions.
- A record of checks, defects, and repairs.

<Accordion title="Need a hint?">
Search source for repeated IDs and nested interactive controls. Correct those before adding one accurate srcset. Keep dimensions and the intended display slot distinct from candidate file widths.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to review your attempt">
Use each completion criterion to demonstrate the result in your source and browser. Explain why your elements and attributes fit the task. Different valid implementations can meet the same requirements. If a criterion is not met, revise and test again before continuing.
</Accordion>

## Test your work

| Check | Action | Expected evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| File dimensions | Inspect the supplied image files. | The small file is 480 × 270 and the wide file is 960 × 540. |
| Selected image | Observe currentSrc and Network at two viewport sizes. | A supplied candidate loads; the notes record conditions rather than assuming a universal choice. |
| Structure | Review IDs and link markup on every page. | No repeated IDs or known nested interactive elements remain; state the scope of your review. |

Record your own observed results beside these expectations. If a check fails, revisit [the relevant lesson](/web-development/frontend/html/advanced/a02-responsive-image-markup), repair your source, and repeat it.

<Accordion title="View a suggested checkpoint solution">

```html
<section id="learning-approach">
  <h2>How I learn</h2>
  <p>I learn a concept, practise it, then build a project.</p>
  <img src="images/study-small.png"
       srcset="images/study-small.png 480w, images/study-wide.png 960w"
       sizes="480px" width="480" height="270"
       alt="Three learning stages: learn, practise, build">
  <p><a href="projects.html">Explore my HTML projects</a></p>
</section>
```

This example gives the browser resolution choices for a fixed 480px slot; it does not change composition at a breakpoint. The introductory image has no lazy-loading hint because it is needed immediately. If an image appears much later in your actual page, justify lazy loading there. Keep source-review notes for all other pages, too.

[Download the complete advanced stage example](/assets/html/portfolio-advanced-solution.zip) to compare connected pages after making your own attempt. It demonstrates one approach; your words and structure may differ where they meet the criteria.

</Accordion>
