## Skill to demonstrate

Review a website and explain the evidence and limits of the review.

Complete this module's lessons before attempting the task. Work from your own understanding first; you may revisit explanations when needed. Keep your checkpoint work alongside your portfolio files.

## Practice materials

Use your existing portfolio files and, when needed, the [supplied local assets](/assets/html/html-practice-assets.zip). Complete the related topic exercises before this checkpoint.

## Your task

Audit every portfolio page. Check markup, keyboard navigation, headings, links, image alternatives, media, and form behaviour. Record issues, repairs, and remaining limitations. Demonstrate a visitor journey across the site.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] The audit covers every page and records reproducible checks.
- [ ] Discovered issues are repaired or explicitly documented.
- [ ] The demonstration includes navigation and practice-form behaviour.
- [ ] Explain why markup validity alone does not establish accessibility.

## Evidence to keep

- Your HTML files and required local assets.
- A short explanation of your decisions.
- A record of checks, defects, and repairs.

<Accordion title="Need a hint?">
List every HTML file first, including result and note pages. Give each check a reproducible action. Deliberately break one link in a backup, capture the failure, repair the saved source, and repeat the exact test.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to review your attempt">
Use each completion criterion to demonstrate the result in your source and browser. Explain why your elements and attributes fit the task. Different valid implementations can meet the same requirements. If a criterion is not met, revise and test again before continuing.
</Accordion>

## Test your work

| Check | Action | Expected evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coverage | Compare your file list with the report. | Every page has an entry, including supporting pages. |
| Repair | Repeat the documented broken-link test after fixing it. | The expected destination is reached from the saved source. |
| Honesty | Review each Pass or Met claim. | Each claim has observed evidence; missing checks are labelled Not tested. |

Record your own observed results beside these expectations. If a check fails, revisit [the relevant lesson](/web-development/frontend/html/advanced/a08-validate-and-audit-a-complete-website), repair your source, and repeat it.
