## Learning scope

Content categories at a practical level; invalid nesting; interactive elements inside interactive elements; duplicate IDs; browser repair of invalid markup.

## Practice challenge

Investigate and repair a supplied document with a nested button/link, a paragraph containing a section, and duplicate IDs.

## Completion criteria

Invalid structures are corrected; IDs are unique; learner explains why a page displaying does not prove its markup is valid.

## Content to develop

- Step-by-step explanation and code examples.
- Optional hints.
- Separately revealed suggested solution with explanation.
