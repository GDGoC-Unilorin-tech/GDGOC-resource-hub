## Learning scope

Markup validation; manual keyboard and content review; link and asset checks; separating validity from accessibility; documenting repairs.

## Practice challenge

Audit a supplied multi-page site, repair its HTML defects, and produce a short findings checklist.

## Completion criteria

Listed markup defects are repaired; keyboard checks are recorded; links and media are checked; learner states the limits of their audit.

## Content to develop

- Step-by-step explanation and code examples.
- Optional hints.
- Separately revealed suggested solution with explanation.
