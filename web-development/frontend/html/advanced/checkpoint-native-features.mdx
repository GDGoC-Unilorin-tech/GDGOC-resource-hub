## Skill to demonstrate

Use native features and metadata with clear limits.

Complete this module's lessons before attempting the task. Work from your own understanding first; you may revisit explanations when needed. Keep your checkpoint work alongside your portfolio files.

## Practice materials

Use your existing portfolio files and, when needed, the [supplied local assets](/assets/html/html-practice-assets.zip). Complete the related topic exercises before this checkpoint.

## Your task

Add a portfolio FAQ using details and summary, improve page metadata, and demonstrate a titled iframe containing a local project-note page with a direct-link alternative. Review the practice contact form and describe the backend and security work needed before accepting real submissions.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] Disclosure summaries are meaningful and keyboard-operable.
- [ ] The iframe has a descriptive title and working alternative link.
- [ ] Titles, descriptions, and language information match each page.
- [ ] The form does not claim that POST, password inputs, or browser validation provide security by themselves.

## Evidence to keep

- Your HTML files and required local assets.
- A short explanation of your decisions.
- A record of checks, defects, and repairs.

<Accordion title="Need a hint?">
Keep the disclosure questions meaningful and the form limitation visible. Create the note page first, then embed it. Review titles and descriptions across all pages, not just the example.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to review your attempt">
Use each completion criterion to demonstrate the result in your source and browser. Explain why your elements and attributes fit the task. Different valid implementations can meet the same requirements. If a criterion is not met, revise and test again before continuing.
</Accordion>

## Test your work

| Check | Action | Expected evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Disclosures | Tab to each summary and toggle with Enter or Space. | Every answer can be opened and closed without a mouse. |
| Embedded note | Read the frame and follow the direct link. | Both display the note; the frame has a descriptive title. |
| Document context | Open each page and inspect its head. | Page-specific titles and descriptions match the content. |
| Form boundary | Explain what POST and required do. | Neither supplies encryption, processing, or trustworthy server-side validation by itself. |

Record your own observed results beside these expectations. If a check fails, revisit [the relevant lesson](/web-development/frontend/html/advanced/a07-form-boundaries-and-safe-practice), repair your source, and repeat it.

```html
<section>

  <iframe src="project-notes.html" title="HTML portfolio implementation notes"
          width="480" height="240" sandbox loading="lazy"></iframe>
  <p><a href="project-notes.html">Read the notes separately</a></p>
</section>
```

Use this body snippet on projects.html. Create a complete project-notes.html document containing a title and main heading, with notes explaining that real message delivery needs a receiving service and server validation. Give each portfolio page its own title and description, and keep the correct html lang value. The stage download includes a complete variant using a separate frame-note.html document.

[Download the complete advanced stage example](/assets/html/portfolio-advanced-solution.zip) to compare connected pages after making your own attempt. It demonstrates one approach; your words and structure may differ where they meet the criteria.
