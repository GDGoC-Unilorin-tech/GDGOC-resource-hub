Your Beginner portfolio already has connected pages, text, lists, and images. In this stage you will turn it into a five-page portfolio with meaningful regions, a learning log, a practice contact form, and accessible media.

## Before you begin

Complete the [previous stage project](/web-development/frontend/html/beginner/project). You should be able to explain your existing pages, open them locally, and repair a broken relative link. Copy your portfolio folder before starting this stage.

## What you will be able to do

- Choose semantic regions and headings that match your content.
- Navigate and test pages with a keyboard, including a working skip link.
- Represent data, quotations, code, and dates with suitable markup.
- Build labelled forms with grouped choices, instructions, and native validation.
- Provide native media controls, timed text, and a readable transcript.

## Prepare your practice folder

Keep `index.html` and `about.html`, then use `projects.html`, `learning-log.html`, and `contact.html` for the growing portfolio. Supporting destinations such as `practice-result.html` and project notes stay beside these pages. Create any page before linking to it.

[Download the practice assets](/assets/html/html-practice-assets.zip) and copy its `images` and `media` folders into your project. The assets are supplied locally; you do not need to find images or record video. Keep names and relative paths consistent. The media lesson explains how to serve files locally for timed-text tests.

<Tip>Use the same editor and browser as before. Save each example in your practice HTML files. The hub's code blocks are teaching examples; they do not automatically edit or run your project.</Tip>

## How to practise and demonstrate

1. Read the lesson, type the example, and predict its result.
2. Run the observation exercise and attempt the challenge.
3. Use hints when needed; reveal the suggested solution after your own attempt.
4. Complete the module checkpoint and keep source files plus test notes.
5. Combine the modules in the [stage project](/web-development/frontend/html/intermediate/project).
