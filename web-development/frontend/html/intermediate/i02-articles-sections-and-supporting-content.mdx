## Learning scope

`article`, `section`, `aside`; section headings; choosing elements based on content relationships.

## Practice challenge

Mark up a news page with two self-contained stories and related supporting information.

## Completion criteria

Articles make sense independently; sections have meaningful headings; supporting content is placed appropriately.

## Content to develop

- Step-by-step explanation and code examples.
- Optional hints.
- Separately revealed suggested solution with explanation.
