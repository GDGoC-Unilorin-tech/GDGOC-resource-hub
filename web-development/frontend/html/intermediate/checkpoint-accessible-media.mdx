## Skill to demonstrate

Provide usable media with controls, captions, and a transcript.

Complete this module's lessons before attempting the task. Work from your own understanding first; you may revisit explanations when needed. Keep your checkpoint work alongside your portfolio files.

## Practice materials

Use your existing portfolio files and, when needed, the [supplied local assets](/assets/html/html-practice-assets.zip). Complete the related topic exercises before this checkpoint.

## Your task

Add the supplied silent learning video using native controls, its WebVTT visual-description track, and the supplied transcript. Test it over HTTP as explained in the media lesson. No recording is required.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] Video and caption paths resolve.
- [ ] Native controls work with the keyboard.
- [ ] Timed text matches the visual stages and timing; the transcript explains the absence of audio.
- [ ] A readable transcript is available.
- [ ] Media does not require autoplay.

## Evidence to keep

- Your HTML files and required local assets.
- A short explanation of your decisions.
- A record of checks, defects, and repairs.

<Accordion title="Need a hint?">
Copy images and media from the supplied pack without renaming individual files. Serve the whole project over HTTP. Check the player, text track, and visible transcript separately.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to review your attempt">
Use each completion criterion to demonstrate the result in your source and browser. Explain why your elements and attributes fit the task. Different valid implementations can meet the same requirements. If a criterion is not met, revise and test again before continuing.
</Accordion>

## Test your work

| Check | Action | Expected evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Playback | Play and pause using the native controls. | The supplied animation plays without forced autoplay. |
| Timed text | Enable English descriptions and watch all six seconds over HTTP. | Descriptions correspond to Learn, Practise, and Build. |
| Alternative | Read the transcript and open the download link. | The transcript describes the sequence and silence; the media file resolves. |

Record your own observed results beside these expectations. If a check fails, revisit [the relevant lesson](/web-development/frontend/html/intermediate/i09-audio-video-and-captions), repair your source, and repeat it.

<Accordion title="View a suggested checkpoint solution">

```html
<section>
      <h2>My learning approach</h2>
      <video controls preload="metadata" width="640" height="360" poster="images/study-wide.png">
        <source src="media/learning.webm" type="video/webm">
        <track src="media/learning-en.vtt" kind="captions" srclang="en" label="English descriptions" default>
        Video playback is unavailable.
      </video>
      <p><a href="media/learning.webm">Download the silent practice video</a></p>
      <h3>Transcript and visual description</h3>
      <p>The animation displays Learn for two seconds, Practise for two seconds, then Build for two seconds. It has no speech or other audio.</p>
    </section>
```

Place this section inside the projects page main region. Copy the media and images directories from the practice pack. The transcript describes a silent visual sequence; spoken media would require captions for speech and meaningful sounds.

[Download the complete intermediate stage example](/assets/html/portfolio-intermediate-solution.zip) to compare connected pages after making your own attempt. It demonstrates one approach; your words and structure may differ where they meet the criteria.

</Accordion>
