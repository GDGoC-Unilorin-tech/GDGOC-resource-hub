## Skill to demonstrate

Present tabular data, dates, quotations, and code meaningfully.

Complete this module's lessons before attempting the task. Work from your own understanding first; you may revisit explanations when needed. Keep your checkpoint work alongside your portfolio files.

## Practice materials

Use your existing portfolio files and, when needed, the [supplied local assets](/assets/html/html-practice-assets.zip). Complete the related topic exercises before this checkpoint.

## Your task

Add a learning log to your portfolio with a captioned study schedule table, a machine-readable date, a quotation attributed to its source, and an HTML snippet displayed as text.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] Table headers and scope describe the data.
- [ ] The date has a matching datetime value.
- [ ] Quotation markup and attribution suit the example.
- [ ] The code snippet displays instead of becoming page markup.

## Evidence to keep

- Your HTML files and required local assets.
- A short explanation of your decisions.
- A record of checks, defects, and repairs.

<Accordion title="Need a hint?">
Use a simple three-column grid before considering merged cells. Add the quotation attribution outside blockquote and escape the code example’s angle brackets.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to review your attempt">
Use each completion criterion to demonstrate the result in your source and browser. Explain why your elements and attributes fit the task. Different valid implementations can meet the same requirements. If a criterion is not met, revise and test again before continuing.
</Accordion>

## Test your work

| Check | Action | Expected evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Table | Choose the Forms cell and identify its headers. | Tuesday is the row header and Topic is the column header. |
| Code and date | View the page, then inspect source. | The th example is displayed as text; datetime matches the visible date. |

Record your own observed results beside these expectations. If a check fails, revisit [the relevant lesson](/web-development/frontend/html/intermediate/i04-data-tables), repair your source, and repeat it.

<Accordion title="View a suggested checkpoint solution">

```html
<h1>My learning log</h1>
<table>
  <caption>Weekly HTML practice</caption>
  <thead><tr><th scope="col">Day</th><th scope="col">Topic</th><th scope="col">Task</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><th scope="row">Monday</th><td>Tables</td><td>Build a study log</td></tr>
    <tr><th scope="row">Tuesday</th><td>Forms</td><td>Label each field</td></tr>
    <tr><th scope="row">Friday</th><td>Review</td><td>Repair broken links</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
<article>
  <h2>My table practice</h2>
  <p><time datetime="2026-10-21">21 October 2026</time></p>
  <blockquote><p>I understand a topic better after building an example.</p></blockquote>
  <p>Original practice quotation from my <cite>Learning Journal</cite>.</p>
  <pre><code>&lt;th scope="col"&gt;Topic&lt;/th&gt;</code></pre>
</article>
```

Place this inside the learning-log main region. The quotation is original sample text, not a statement attributed to a real person. Use your own date and journal entry, keeping the visible and machine-readable dates consistent.

[Download the complete intermediate stage example](/assets/html/portfolio-intermediate-solution.zip) to compare connected pages after making your own attempt. It demonstrates one approach; your words and structure may differ where they meet the criteria.

</Accordion>
