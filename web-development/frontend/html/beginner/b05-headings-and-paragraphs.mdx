## What you will learn

- Organise content into headings and paragraphs.
- Choose heading levels by hierarchy.
- Distinguish a heading from bold text.

## Before you begin

Complete the previous lesson in this module. Keep your editor and browser ready.

<Tip>Examples labelled as body snippets go inside your existing document's body, not inside the MDX source of a live website. Complete HTML documents can be saved directly as .html files. Read the explanation for any head-only or intentionally broken examples.</Tip>

## Headings outline the page

`h1` through `h6` identify headings from highest to lowest level. For this course, give each page one clear `h1`. Main sections use `h2`; a subsection within one of those sections uses `h3`.

Choose levels by the relationship between ideas, not the font size you want. Browsers and assistive technologies use that structure to help people navigate.

## Paragraphs hold related sentences

Use `p` for a paragraph. Create a new paragraph when the idea changes. A line break in your source usually becomes ordinary whitespace, so pressing Enter repeatedly in the editor does not create new paragraphs in the browser.

Avoid putting a heading or another paragraph inside a `p`. They are separate parts of the document.

## Worked example

```html
<h1>About Amina</h1>
<p>I am learning to build websites.</p>
<h2>Background</h2>
<p>I enjoy solving problems with other students.</p>
<h2>Learning goals</h2>
<h3>This month</h3>
<p>I want to complete the HTML course.</p>
```

The two h2 headings are peers. “This month” is a subsection of “Learning goals”, so it uses h3. Paste the snippet inside your existing body.

<Note>Do not choose h4 just to make text smaller. Styling belongs in CSS.</Note>

## Practice challenge

Create a learner profile with Background, Interests, and Goals sections. Put Short-term goals and Long-term goals beneath Goals. Write at least one paragraph in every section.

Attempt the task first. Save your source and inspect the result in your browser. Record what you expected and what you observed.

<Accordion title="Need a hint?">
Sketch an outline first. Use the same level for ideas at the same depth.
</Accordion>

## Completion checklist

- [ ] One clear h1 identifies the page.
- [ ] Main sections use h2 and goal subsections use h3.
- [ ] Every section contains a paragraph.
- [ ] Headings and paragraphs are not nested inside paragraphs.

<Accordion title="View a suggested solution">

```html
<h1>My learner profile</h1>
<h2>Background</h2>
<p>I am a student exploring web development.</p>
<h2>Interests</h2>
<p>I enjoy reading and making things.</p>
<h2>Goals</h2>
<p>I want to share useful ideas through websites.</p>
<h3>Short-term goals</h3>
<p>Finish my first HTML project.</p>
<h3>Long-term goals</h3>
<p>Contribute to a community product.</p>
```

This implementation follows the requirements above. Compare it with your own attempt against each checklist item. Your wording and other valid choices may differ. Explain one decision you made and revise any unmet criterion.
