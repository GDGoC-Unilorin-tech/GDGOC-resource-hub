## Skill to demonstrate

Create, open, and explain a complete HTML document.

Complete this module's lessons before attempting the task. Work from your own understanding first; you may revisit explanations when needed. Keep your checkpoint work alongside your portfolio files.

## Practice materials

Use your existing portfolio files and, when needed, the [supplied local assets](/assets/html/html-practice-assets.zip). Complete the related topic exercises before this checkpoint.

## Your task

Create a new file named checkpoint.html without copying a complete document. Include the document skeleton, language, encoding, viewport, a descriptive tab title, a heading, and a paragraph. Open it in your browser and change the paragraph.

## Completion criteria

- [ ] The file opens and the saved edit appears after refresh.
- [ ] The document has a doctype, html, head, and body in the correct relationship.
- [ ] Language, encoding, viewport, and title are appropriate.
- [ ] Explain the difference between the document title and visible heading.

## Evidence to keep

- Your HTML files and required local assets.
- A short explanation of your decisions.
- A record of checks, defects, and repairs.

<Accordion title="Need a hint?">
Break the task into the concepts covered by this module. Test one change at a time. Revisit the related lesson examples, then return to your own implementation.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to review your attempt">
Use each completion criterion to demonstrate the result in your source and browser. Explain why your elements and attributes fit the task. Different valid implementations can meet the same requirements. If a criterion is not met, revise and test again before continuing.
</Accordion>

<Note>This checkpoint records self-assessment unless a reviewer checks your evidence. It does not automatically verify your code or unlock the next module. A complete suggested implementation is not included in this assessment; related topic exercises provide worked examples.</Note>
